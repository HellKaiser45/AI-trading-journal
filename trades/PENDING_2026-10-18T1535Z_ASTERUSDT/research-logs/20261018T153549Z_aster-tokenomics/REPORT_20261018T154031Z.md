# Short answer

Aster is a YZi Labs-backed multi-chain perp DEX (Astherus + APX Finance/ApolloX merger, rebranded Mar 2025; token TGE 2025-09-17; BEP-20 on BNB Chain, now also on its own L1 "Aster Chain" since 2026-03-17). 8B max supply; officially allocated 53.5% airdrops / 30% ecosystem & community / 7% treasury (locked) / 5% team (locked) / 4.5% liquidity & listing (unlocked at TGE). As of 2026-10-18: circulating **2,713,596,422** (33.9% of 8B), total **7,792,632,673** (~207.37M burned, 2.6%), price $0.694, mcap $1.88B (rank 50), FDV $5.41B (post-burn) / $5.57B (8B basis), TVL **$728.2M**. The model shifted from an airdrop-emission engine to a fee-funded deflation model (June 17, 2026 upgrade): 99% of daily fees TWAP-buy back ASTER for veASTER stakers, with a 1:1 burn from reserve (team allocation first), bi-weekly, targeting a 3B total supply. Dilution pressure is currently low and mechanical: team locked until 2027-09-17, treasury locked, staking emissions only ~450k ASTER/week; the dominant risks are discretionary airdrop-reserve distribution, float growth via buyback→staker distributions, fee dependence, and an integrity/competition overhang.

# Key concepts

- **Allocation split (official, of 8B max):** Airdrop 53.5% (4.28B), Ecosystem & Community 30% (2.4B), Treasury 7% (560M), Team 5% (400M), Liquidity & Listing 4.5% (360M). No visible VC/investor bucket — unusually community-weighted.
- **veASTER staking:** lock ASTER (up to 208 weeks ≈ 4 years) with a validator; two reward layers — Base APY pool 150,000 ASTER/week (validator performance share) and Loyalty Rewards 300,000 ASTER/epoch (weekly) + variable fee-buyback component; early exit penalized.
- **Fee buyback → burn ("198%"):** 99% of daily platform fees buy ASTER via TWAP into public wallet 0xa0edBaBc…A1b60; bought tokens are distributed to veASTER stakers; an equal amount is burned from reserve (team allocation burned first) bi-weekly until total supply reaches 3B. "198%" is the project's label for 99% buyback + 99% burn — a mechanism descriptor, not a yield.
- **Emission cut:** the Ecosystem & Community 20-month linear vesting ran only Oct 2025–Jan 2026, then was replaced by the much smaller staking-emission model (third parties describe a ~97% cut "earlier in 2026"; a specific Mar 30, 2026 date circulating on trackers is à vérifier).
- **Burn ≠ float reduction:** burns remove tokens from *locked reserves* (team first), so they shrink total supply but not circulating float; simultaneously, buyback distributions hand tokens to stakers who may sell — float tends to grow even as total supply falls.
- **Cliff vesting:** team allocation has a cliff (now extended 12 months to 2027-09-17), then 40 months linear at 10M ASTER/month.
- **FDV vs mcap:** FDV uses post-burn total supply (7.79B → $5.41B); using the 8B max gives $5.57B. mcap/FDV ratio ≈ 0.35 → 65% of supply value still outside circulation.

# Suggested angles (for the ASTERUSDT LONG, entry 0.691, expire 2026-10-20T21:00Z)

1. **No scheduled unlock in the trade window.** Team locked to 2027-09-17, treasury locked, emissions ~450k/wk. The mechanical dilution argument is currently neutral-to-bullish; the next hard-dated supply event is the 2027-09-17 team cliff (10M/month, ~0.4% of max supply monthly).
2. **Trade the fee pulse, not the burn slogan.** Buybacks scale with fees (~3–4M ASTER per 2 weeks recently and falling); staking APY already halved in two weeks (6.41→4.43% / 39.48→23.79%). If perp volume decays, both the deflation story and the staking bid weaken — fee/volume trend is the leading indicator.
3. **Float math cuts both ways.** Burns shrink locked reserve, not float; buyback distributions *add* to float. Net effect: circulating share rises toward the locked 5.08B (≈$3.5B at $0.69) — FDV/mcap gap ~2.9× is the real overhang. A sustained uptrend requires fee-funded absorption to outrun staker distribution.
4. **The 3B target is aspirational, not scheduled.** 207M burned in ~13 months (2.6% of max) vs 5B to go — at current pace this takes decades; discount marketing framing ("198%", "62.5% supply cut").
5. **Watch-list for invalidation/confirmation:** DefiLlama TVL trend ($728M), perp-volume share vs Hyperliquid, biweekly burn updates (aster buyback reports), any governance vote redirecting fees or releasing airdrop reserves, CZ-linked disclosures.
