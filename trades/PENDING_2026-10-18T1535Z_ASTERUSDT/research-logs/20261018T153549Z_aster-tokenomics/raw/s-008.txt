"Important upgrade [June 17th]: Aster's tokenomics upgrade puts the platform's own activity to work, rewarding veASTER stakers and reducing $ASTER supply. 99% of daily fees buy back $ASTER automatically, all of which is distributed to veASTER stakers. Every buyback triggers an equal burn from reserve. Burns are executed bi-weekly and will continue until the total supply reaches 3,000,000,000."

Allocation table (of 8B max supply):
- Airdrop 53.5% — 4,280,000,000 ASTER
- Ecosystem & Community 30% — 2,400,000,000 ASTER
- Treasury 7% — 560,000,000 ASTER
- Team 5% — 400,000,000 ASTER
- Liquidity & Listings 4.5% — 360,000,000 ASTER

"Airdrop (53.5% - 4,280,000,000 $ASTER): 8.8% of total allocation (704,000,000 $ASTER) will be unlocked immediately upon Aster TGE for eligible participants from two reward programs: Aster Spectra and Aster Gems. Unclaimed $ASTER tokens will be redirected back to Airdrop & Community Rewards allocation."

"This allocation (Ecosystem & Community, excluding APX-to-ASTER swap) was originally vested over 20 months on a linear distribution model. The linear vesting was active for 4 months (October 2025 – January 2026) and has since been replaced by a staking emission model. Staking rewards are currently the sole active use of this allocation."

"Treasury (7% - 560,000,000 $ASTER): It will not enter circulating supply after TGE and remains fully locked until utilized via governance-approved mechanisms."

"Team (5% - 400,000,000 $ASTER): The cliff on the Team allocation will be extended by 12 months, from September 17, 2026 to September 17, 2027. Under the original schedule, 10,000,000 $ASTER was set to vest each month beginning September 17, 2026. The allocation has remained fully locked since TGE and will remain so until September 17, 2027. Cliff: 12 months from September 17, 2027. Vesting: 40 months of linear distribution (10,000,000 $ASTER per month)."

"Liquidity & Listing (4.5% - 360,000,000 $ASTER): This allocation will be fully unlocked upon TGE."

"Fee Buyback: 99% of Aster's daily platform fees are used to automatically buy back $ASTER. The buyback runs via TWAP across each day and settles on-chain to the public buyback wallet (0xa0edBaBcb48034e368de286b49F9603C7AfA1b60), with no discretionary reserve. Burn Mechanism: For every $ASTER bought back, an equal amount is burned from reserve — with the Team allocation burned first. Burns are executed bi-weekly and will continue until the total supply reaches 3,000,000,000."
