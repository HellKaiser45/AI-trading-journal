"Dual Reward Structure ... Weekly Reward Pool:
- Base APY | Validator transaction share and your delegation size | 150,000 $ASTER
- Loyalty Rewards | Lock duration and trading activity | 300,000 $ASTER + Fee Buyback rewards (variable)

The current maximum lock duration is 208 weeks (approximately 4 years).
Epoch: reward settlement cycle (currently weekly).

New as of June 17, 2026: Loyalty Rewards now include a variable component on top of the 300,000 $ASTER base. Each epoch, all $ASTER bought back from platform fees is added to the Loyalty Rewards pool and distributed to veASTER holders by lock weight.

99% of daily platform fees flow into the buyback wallet (0xa0edBaBcb48034e368de286b49F9603C7AfA1b60); buybacks execute via TWAP across each day and settle on-chain. For every $ASTER bought back, an equal amount is burned from reserve — burns execute bi-weekly and continue until total supply reaches 3,000,000,000.

Each permissionless Spot listing incurs a 50,000 USDT fee, which is also used to buy back $ASTER as additional staking rewards (listing fee wallet 0x39C473f4420e4ae9Ab3fe9e7ceDFc08F9684bB1a).
