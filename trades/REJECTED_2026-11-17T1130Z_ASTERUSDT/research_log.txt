DECISION: NOT TAKEN. JEV no_ticket 0.91.
The LONG candidate was contradicted on the requirement that positive expectancy must not
rest solely on the limit-entry discount. It does. A single-sided ticket on a two-sided
harvest is half the measured expectancy at full single-side variance, and the journal
cannot hold both sides (sizing and cross-trade correlation are out of scope).
