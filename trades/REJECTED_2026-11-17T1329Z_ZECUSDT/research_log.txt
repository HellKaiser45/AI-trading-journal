THESIS
ZEC has fallen -44.1% from its 2026-09-26 high of 1699.00 and is sitting -32.0% under its
20-bar high while +18.7% off the 800.67 low, back above SMA20 but still under SMA50. The
thesis I tested: a deep drawdown that has already bounced off its low and reclaimed the
20-bar mean is a buyable state on 4h, with a limit 3% below the close, TP +10%, SL -8%.
Invalidation: a 4h close back under SMA20 (902.52) kills the reclaim.

WHAT WAS TESTED
Signal: dd20 <= -28% AND bounce off the 20-bar low >= 15% AND close > SMA20 AND
close < SMA50, one episode per 30 bars. It fires on 0.117% of 144,000 scanned 4h bars.

1. AUDITED TICKET, LONG limit -3% / TP +10% / SL -8% / 30 bars (120h). Fill model as the
   manual defines it: limit on first touch; after that TP and SL at their level on touch;
   a bar touching both is a LOSS; in the fill bar only SL counts; otherwise close of the
   last bar. Pooled 12 symbols: 141 fills, EV per fill +0.820%, median +0.593%,
   TP 51 / SL 54 / EXP 36. ZEC alone: 17 episodes, 16 fills, EV per fill +2.753%,
   median +10.000%.
2. SHORT MIRROR on the very same signal bars (limit +3%, TP -10%, SL +8%): pooled
   +1.991%; ZEC alone +0.868%. THE MIRROR BEATS THE LONG.
3. LONG minus SHORT per symbol: LONG wins on only 4 of 12 (DOGE +0.507, TRX +3.903,
   UNI +0.455, ZEC +1.885 pp). ETH -7.912, BNB -7.989, LINK -3.849, ADA -2.229,
   LTC -1.918, AVAX -1.715, SOL -0.803, XRP -0.795 pp.
4. Threshold sensitivity of the LONG-SHORT gap, 15 combinations from dd<=-20/bo>=10 to
   dd<=-35/bo>=20: the gap is NEGATIVE in 13 of 15 (from -0.129 to -6.641 pp) and
   positive only in two geometry variants at dd<=-28 (tp10/sl12 +0.434, tp15/sl10 +0.694).
   A sign that flips with the stop distance is not a direction.
5. ZEC's own strictness ladder: LONG +0.770 / +1.659 / +2.753 / +2.270 / +1.000 / +2.286
   against SHORT +0.221 / +0.755 / +0.868 / +1.550 / +5.500 / +6.400 for dd<=-20 / -25 /
   -28 / -30 / -35 / -40. The short side overtakes the long exactly where the drawdown
   gets deepest.
6. ADJACENT-BAR CONTROL, same symbols, same days, +/-1 to 3 bars (4 to 12 hours away):
   pooled EV per fill +2.235% versus the signal bar's +0.820%. On ZEC alone +3.022%
   versus +2.753%. A bar a few hours earlier paid MORE.
7. Unconditional baseline, every bar of every panel: n=98,685 fills, EV per fill
   -0.092%. So the state does beat the unconditional baseline - but the mirror, the
   adjacent bars and the drift all beat it by more.
8. Clustering: the 168 episode-rows collapse to 78 distinct calendar days. Per-day mean
   EV +2.282%, 59% of days positive, bootstrap by day CI95 [+0.723, +3.872],
   P(EV<=0) 0.001. The state is a real volatility-cluster marker. It is not a direction.
9. BETA CONFOUND: ZEC went from 140.21 to 942.27 (+572%) across the panel; the
   unconditional 120h LONG drift on ZEC alone is +1.46% mean. Most of the headline
   +2.753% is the instrument's drift, not the state.
10. MAE audit of the SL: of 141 fills the worst excursion beyond entry reached -13.36%,
    and 54 of them traded through -8%. The "zero SL" impression from any single-symbol
    sample does not survive the pooled data.

ERRORS I MADE IN THIS RUN, ALL CORRECTED BEFORE DECIDING
- Several intermediate scripts printed a "LONG vs SHORT gap" computed as LONG minus SHORT
  on the same forward return. Because SHORT equals -LONG on that basis, the number is
  exactly 2x LONG and measures NOTHING about direction. Every such figure is void. The
  only valid comparison is LONG minus SHORT where each side is measured against its OWN
  baseline, which is what section 3 reports.
- Two fill implementations disagreed: one let TP count inside the fill bar, which the
  manual forbids. That inflated the pooled number from +0.820% to +3.705%. The audited
  model in section 1 is the one used for the decision.
- A first pass also treated a 2,442-bar ASTER panel as a full history; the panel is
  2,442 bars against 12,000 for the others, so the out-of-sample test that started from
  it had 11 usable symbols, not 12.

LEARNING PASS (27 prior folders, arithmetic sum -9.04%, win rate 15.4%)
- The dominant failure mode is a stop too tight inside the leg (6 of 9 losses). SL -8%
  here is 2.32x ATR and clears the 800.67 low, so that failure mode is not the reason
  this one is rejected.
- LOSS-1.50%_2026-10-23T1147Z_ZECUSDT lesson 1: a limit filled by a fast one-way bar is
  adverse selection. Not applicable here (the limit is 3% away and unfilled at decision
  time) but it is why the ticket uses a 0.94 measured fill rate rather than a near-market
  entry.
- EXPIRED+3.95%_2026-10-18T1535Z_ASTERUSDT lesson 1: never put the TP on an extreme the
  market already rejected. TP 1014.09 sits inside the 975-1015 supply shelf and under the
  11-13 swing high of 991.97, so this lesson is respected.
- EXPIRED_2026-11-01T1010Z_ASTERUSDT lesson 1: quote the far edge of a band for fill
  robustness. The limit at 921.90 is under the 900-960 box floor.

WHAT COULD NOT BE ESTABLISHED

  analogue inside the sample, so the state cannot be calibrated for a post-parabolic tape.
