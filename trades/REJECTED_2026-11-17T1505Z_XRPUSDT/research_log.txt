exchange series printed the lowest low near 2026-11-14T22:00-23:00Z. The replica is an
eyeball reading of the supplied image and the manual forbids fitting it to exchange
data, so it is left as read. The error means the replica cannot be used to time
anything; all statistics above come from the candle files, not from the replica.

BUGS FOUND IN THIS RUN, ALL VOIDED
1. Two adjacency scripts (t5, t6) evaluated the state at bar i+off while entering at
   bar i. For positive offsets that is look-ahead. Their +3.13/+5.64/+9.45 gap figures
   are discarded.
2. One script (t9) reused the loop variable `st` inside a per-symbol loop, so the XRP
   geometry sweep and era table were computed on ZEC episode indices applied to XRP
   candles. Both tables discarded.
3. One pooled confidence interval (t8) repeated per-symbol means instead of per-event
   values, fabricating a narrow interval around the mean of 14 numbers. Discarded.
4. In the first draft of the chart generator the prediction SVG was emitted with two
   root <svg> elements and failed to parse. Rewritten; both SVGs are now validated with
   xml.dom.minidom.parse before being written.
