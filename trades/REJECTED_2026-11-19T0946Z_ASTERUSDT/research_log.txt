WHAT I GOT WRONG ALONG THE WAY (kept, not buried)
- My first pre-specified state (crash + bounce + ATR coil <=p30) did NOT fire on the live
  bar: ATR percentile is 68.3, not <=30. I did not adjust the threshold to make it fire;
  I reported the state as not met and defined a different, honestly-named state.
- My first beta control returned +17.9% for non-event bars, which is nonsense: it sampled
  a different period and inherited a bull leg. Replaced with a symbol-matched control
  built from the same symbol and window.
- I suspected the bounce was an intrabar artifact and tested it: only 1.9% of panel
  bounces are intrabar. The hypothesis was wrong and I discarded it.
- The 0.6-ATR/3-ATR geometry I tried first was an arbitrary grid; the whole grid is
  reported above, including the cells that lost.
- ASTER's 0.8155 wick is unexplained. I clamped it rather than pretending it is real.
